# ResearchFlow AI

### A Multi-Agent Platform for Academic Workflow and Research Paper Intelligence

ResearchFlow AI is a proposed multi-agent platform designed to support students in managing their academic workflow and research activities through coordinated AI agents.

The system combines academic workflow management with research paper intelligence. Specialized AI agents work together to help students discover and analyze research papers, compare existing work, identify research gaps, suggest research directions and methodology, organize citations, review research outputs, and manage academic milestones.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

Students often use multiple disconnected tools for academic planning and research activities. ResearchFlow AI aims to provide an integrated platform where specialized AI agents collaborate through an orchestrator to support the complete academic and research workflow.

The platform follows a multi-agent approach in which different agents perform specialized tasks while sharing relevant student and research context.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### Academic Workflow
- Student academic profile management
- Syllabus and academic task tracking
- Exam and assignment deadline management
- Academic milestone tracking
- Personalized academic workflow support

### Research Paper Intelligence
- Relevant research paper discovery
- Research paper content analysis
- Methodology and dataset extraction
- Results and limitation extraction
- Multi-paper comparison
- Research gap identification
- Research direction suggestions
- Research methodology suggestions
- Citation and reference organization
- Research output review

### Multi-Agent Collaboration
- Orchestrator-based task decomposition
- Specialized AI agents for different research tasks
- Coordination between research and academic workflow agents
- Human-in-the-loop approval of research recommendations

---

## 🤖 Proposed AI Agents

| Agent | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Orchestrator Agent | Breaks research problems into tasks and coordinates agents |
| Literature Agent | Finds relevant research papers |
| Paper Intelligence Agent | Extracts methodology, datasets, results and limitations |
| Comparison Agent | Creates structured comparison of research papers |
| Gap Agent | Identifies research gaps |
| Idea Agent | Suggests possible research directions |
| Methodology Agent | Suggests suitable research methodologies |
| Citation Agent | Organizes research references and citations |
| Reviewer Agent | Checks research output quality and consistency |
| Academic Workflow Agent | Tracks academic milestones and deadlines |

---

## 🏗️ Proposed Workflow

Student Research Problem
        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓
Comparison Agent
        ↓
Gap Agent
        ↓
Idea Agent
        ↓
Methodology Agent
        ↓
Citation Agent
        ↓
Reviewer Agent
        ↓
Final Research Plan / Research Insights
        ↓
Human Approval

The Academic Workflow Agent works alongside the research workflow to track academic milestones, tasks and deadlines.

## 📚 Research Direction

The proposed system is inspired by research on Agentic AI in Education and focuses on applying multi-agent collaboration to academic workflow and research-paper intelligence.

The project aims to explore how specialized AI agents can collaborate, share context and provide structured research and academic recommendations while keeping the student involved in the final decision-making process.
